About

The largest, longest-running and non-profit English high school mock trial platform nationwide.

Peer Potential Mock Trial is a non-profit, student-run platform and the largest, longest-running English high school mock trial community in China. We offer competitions rigorous enough to challenge future attorneys, yet we also carry the mission of making mock trial more accessible for all high schoolers.

Our mentorship program

We offer free coaching to teach beginners from scratch. Our coaches are from the nation’s most prestigious mock trial teams, and our sessions include hands-on, 1V1 feedback for free.

Our community

Joining PPMT means connecting with a vast alumni network. Today, from collegiate mock trial teams at Yale, MIT, and the University of Chicago to law school classrooms at Harvard, Penn, and Columbia, PPMT alumni continue to leave their mark around the world.

Our Story

Peer Potential Mock Trial was founded in 2014 as a way to bring American Mock Trial Association rules to Chinese high schoolers, many of whom competed in English despite not speaking it as a first language. Within a few years of its inception, Peer Potential’s alumni were competing for top-10 American college mock trial programs. A short while later, they were earning admission to top law schools and crediting their high school experiences for their success.

Today, PPMT alumni continue to leave their mark around the world.

Every summer, these students and alumni gather again for a long-awaited reunion — arguing cases, meeting their closest friends, having fun at PPnight, and leaving with far more than just awards.
